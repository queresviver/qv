Glossary

Coinages, concepts and figures

I. Poetics

eternal reportage
(QV’s motto) QV’s practice: reporting events as if the report never ended, because what matters is not the event but what endures of it. PT eterna reportagem / reportagem eterna
human reportage
(Ultra Fiorentina pace) The report only a human can make: discerning “the faintest palpitations of our blood pump”. PT a humana reportagem
reportage as poetry
(Ultra Fiorentina pace) The central thesis: the only form of new poetry is reportage. PT o relato enquanto poesia
the new register
(Ultra Fiorentina pace) The mode of writing that follows from that thesis. PT o novo registo
the new writing
(Manifesto of Aeviternalism) Writing that ignores or despises objectivity; “only those who write poetry may enter”. PT a nova escrita
putative objectivity
(Manifesto of Aeviternalism) The supposed neutrality of journalism, treated as an imposture. PT putativa objectividade
the angel’s gesture
(Manifesto of Aeviternalism) An image of the new writing: exact delicacy, “a finger laid on the hand of a sleeping king”. PT o gesto do anjo
mythopoetic stance
(Ancient Hearts; the Moon) Reportage that makes myth instead of taking it apart. PT postura mito-poética
automatic text
(Ancient Hearts; the Moon) Machine-generated text, “the one born with neither Father nor Mother nor Dog”. PT o texto automático
wieszcz
(Abstract Saints #3) From Polish, the Romantic poet-prophet; QV takes it on as an identity. Stays in italics in every language.

II. Time and permanence

aeviternalism
(Manifesto of Aeviternalism) The “new eternity made word”: from the Latin aevum, the time of angels, between eternity and human time. An eternity in motion, against the immobile. PT Aeviternalismo
the honey of perpetuity
(Manifesto of Aeviternalism) What all beings of all eras produce together; a conductor of consciousnesses. PT o mel da perpetuidade
palimpsest of gravity
(Clarimundo) Creation as layers of errors hovering one over another. PT palimpsesto da gravidade
interior durations / thickness of time
(Crippled Symmetries) Parallel times that repetitive music makes perceptible. PT durações interiores, espessura do tempo
post-inflationary mode
(Crippled Symmetries) Respecting the unreachable horizon through repetition, not growth. PT modo pós-inflacionário

III. The image

the image’s bad luck
(lim Abstract Saints(n)) The image’s misfortune: someone is only venerated after death. PT o azar da imagem
the unknown image
(lim Abstract Saints(n)) The image that abandoned us and that is still fought for. PT a imagem ignota
the Image
(lim Abstract Saints(n)) Capitalised: the abstraction that replaces father and mother in our complexes. PT a Imagem
Visões (x)
(Abstract Saints #4) Layers of memories of faces adding up fractally, “like a sum that never resolves”; variables: the light, the state of the observer, the angle, the openness of the heart. Stays as is in every language.

IV. Crowds and control

Mother Cloud
(Abstract Saints #3) The cloud as deity: where all data will be and from where desire is predicted. PT a Mãe-Nuvem
the vibe (its texture, controlled, rhizomatic)
(Abstract Saints #3) The collective atmosphere; the crisis is its being controlled and not rhizomatic. PT a vibe
nature’s calculated spontaneity
(Abstract Saints #3) The order hidden in apparent chance, which makes it possible to predict leaves and crowds. PT a espontaneidade calculada da natureza
the Lisbon delta
(Abstract Saints #1) Lisbon’s dialect and manner, which foreigners absorb in a flash. PT o delta lisboeta

V. Nature, sound, science

acupuncture of scenes, places and qualia
(Abstract Saints #4) Feasting and memory as exact points that keep life circulating. PT acupuntura de cenas, locais e qualia
the protocol of leaves
(Abstract Saints #3) The fall of leaves as a solvable equation, not a stochastic process. PT o protocolo das folhas
kids’ panpsychism
(Abstract Saints #3) The childhood belief that waves, clouds and roots can be predicted, soon scorned by sceptics. PT pan-psiquismo dos putos
the age of sound / the primordial chord
(The Age of Sound) The question of the genealogy of sound, back to the very first. PT a idade do som, o acorde primordial
nostalgia for the Cryptozoic
(lim Abstract Saints(n)) Longing for the 90% of Earth’s history before visible life. PT a nostalgia pelo Criptozoico
atomic walks through nature’s archives
(Ultra Fiorentina pace) The best catalyst for calm; time well spent. PT caminhadas atómicas pelos arquivos da natureza
festive cardiomegaly
(The Age of Sound) The big hearts of an audience turned participant. PT a festiva cardiomegalia
the grey honey of normality
(The Age of Sound) The daily humiliation of existing in routine. PT o mel cinzento da normalidade

VI. Figures

the Mystagogue of Lightness
(Beverly Glenn-Copeland) The initiator into the mysteries of lightness, who teaches us “to sing the apotheosis of lightness”. PT o Mistagogo da Leveza
the Oracle of 3 Strings
(Bill Orcutt) The guitarist as oracle of suffering-through-the-guitar. PT o Oráculo das 3 Cordas
Our Marble Lady of Mirrors
(Ancient Hearts; the Moon) The old thing par excellence, kept in the safe at the centre of the earth. PT Nossa Marmórea Senhora dos Espelhos
the Croats
(Ancient Hearts; the Moon) The invoked saviours who take away all the mirrors. PT os Croatas
little witch Johanna
(Clarimundo) The brewer of the catacombs, “cynical wart of a person”. PT a pequena bruxa Johanna

VII. Notation

The series as a limit: each text is a term in a sequence tending to infinity.
(n), (x)
Variables in the writing: the series (n) and the visions (x).
ኦሮማይ ትረካ ክፍል 6
(Ultra Fiorentina pace) In Amharic, “Oromai, narrative, part 6”: a homage to Baalu Girma.
